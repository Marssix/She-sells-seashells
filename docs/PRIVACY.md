# Privacy Policy – She Sells Seashells

**Effective: November 19, 2026** (first version: November 12, 2026)

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Discord bot **She Sells Seashells** ("the bot", "we", "us") processes, why, and who it is shared with. The bot is a hobby project, and its source code is publicly available at <https://github.com/Marssix/She-sells-seashells>.

## 1. Data controller and contact
The data controller is the operator of the bot (GitHub user **Marssix**).
For privacy matters, contact us by opening an issue at <https://github.com/Marssix/She-sells-seashells/issues>. Please do not include personal information in the issue. If needed, we will arrange a private way to get in touch.

## 2. Information we process

| Information | Source | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Discord user ID, username and server display name | Discord, when you use a command or button | Running the game: who the seller is, whose turn it is, your name in the result message |
| Server, channel and thread IDs | Discord | Linking the game to the right thread |
| The content of messages you write **in your game thread while the game is running** | Discord | Your sales pitch is passed to the AI buyer so it can respond |
| Use of commands and buttons | Discord | Game actions (starting a game, accepting an offer, giving up) |
| **Voice mode only:** the seller's speech in the voice channel during their own game, and the text it is converted to | Discord voice channel | Your spoken sales pitch is converted to text, shown in the game thread and passed to the AI buyer |
| Game statistics: your Discord user ID, the server ID, and for each finished game the buyer, product, result, sale price, points, whether you found the buyer's weakness, number of turns and end time | Generated by the bot when a game ends | Showing your statistics with `/kauppa stats` and future leaderboards |
| Daily game counter: your Discord user ID, today's date and how many games you have started today | Generated by the bot when you start a game | Enforcing the daily game limit, which protects the operator from excessive AI costs |

Game statistics **do not** include the content of your messages.

**Other messages:** The bot has Discord's *Message Content* permission so it can read sales pitches. Because of this, Discord also delivers other messages from channels the bot can access. The bot immediately ignores every message that is not from the seller of an active game in that game's own thread. Ignored messages are not stored, logged or forwarded.

We **do not collect** email addresses, payment details, location data or any information other than what is listed above.

### Voice mode
When you start a game with `/kauppa soita`, the bot joins your current voice channel, reads the buyer's replies aloud and **listens to you, the seller**, so you can speak your sales pitch. When the bot joins, it posts a notice in the game thread that it is listening.

- **Only the seller is listened to.** The bot processes audio only from the person who started the call. Discord delivers the bot audio from other people in the channel too, but the bot discards it immediately without processing it.
- **Audio is never stored or sent anywhere.** Each spoken turn (ending in about one second of silence) is held in memory only, converted to text with a speech recognition model (Whisper) that runs **on the bot's own server**, and discarded right away. Audio is not recorded, saved to disk, logged or sent to any third party.
- **The recognized text is handled like a typed message.** It is posted in the game thread so you can see what the bot heard, and it is sent to Anthropic as your sales pitch (see section 4).
- Speech shorter than about 0.4 seconds, and speech that starts while the buyer is talking, is ignored.
- Everyone in the voice channel can hear the buyer. The bot leaves the channel when the game ends.

You can still type your sales pitch in the game thread instead of speaking. The operator can turn listening off entirely, in which case the bot joins the channel deafened and receives no audio from anyone.

## 3. Legal basis
Processing is based on **legitimate interest** (EU General Data Protection Regulation, Art. 6(1)(f)). The information is needed to provide the game that you start yourself, and we process only what is necessary for the game to work.

## 4. Who we share information with
- **Anthropic (Claude API):** the AI buyer's replies are generated with Anthropic's Claude model. We send Anthropic **the text of your sales pitch** and the game state (for example, the buyer character, the product and the meter values). Your Discord IDs and name are **not** sent. By default, Anthropic does not use data sent through its API to train its models. Learn more: <https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy>.
- **Discord:** all game messages pass through Discord, which processes them under its own terms: <https://discord.com/privacy>.
- **Microsoft (Azure AI Speech), voice mode only, if the operator has enabled it:** by default, the buyer's replies are turned into speech locally on the bot's own server (Piper), and the text is not sent anywhere. If the operator switches to Microsoft's text-to-speech service instead, we send Microsoft **only the text of the buyer's reply** and the voice settings. No audio from you or anyone else, and no Discord IDs or names, are sent. A reply is written by the AI and may repeat words from your sales pitch. Learn more: <https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement>.
- **Hosting:** the bot runs on the operator's computer or a cloud server, and the hosting provider may technically process the bot's log data.

We do not sell information or use it for advertising.

## 5. Transfers outside the EU
Anthropic and Discord also process data in the United States. If Microsoft's speech service is enabled, it is called in the North Europe (Ireland) region. These transfers rely on the transfer mechanisms approved by the EU that these providers use, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 6. How long information is kept
- **Game state and conversation history** are kept only in the bot's memory. They are deleted when the game ends or the bot restarts.
- **The daily game counter** is kept only for the current day (Finnish time). Counters from earlier days are deleted automatically when the bot starts or when anyone starts a game on a later day. It is not removed by `/kauppa unohda`, because the limit would otherwise be easy to bypass, but it disappears at the end of the day anyway.
- **Voice audio** (voice mode) is kept in memory only for the second or so it takes to convert one spoken turn to text, and is then discarded. It is never written to disk.
- **Game statistics** (see section 2) are stored in the bot's database until you delete them or the bot is shut down permanently. You can delete all of your statistics on a server at any time with the command `/kauppa unohda`.
- **Messages on Discord:** the game thread and its messages stay on Discord according to your server's settings. The bot archives the thread one minute after the game ends. You can delete your own messages on Discord at any time.
- **Technical logs** may contain your display name, the chosen buyer, the game result and error messages. Logs are kept for no more than 30 days for troubleshooting.
- **Anthropic** retains API requests according to its own policies (see section 4).

## 7. Your rights
Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:
- know what information about you is processed
- request that your information be corrected or deleted
- object to the processing
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Finland, this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman: <https://tietosuoja.fi/en>.

The only information the bot stores beyond a single day is your game statistics. You can delete them yourself with `/kauppa unohda`, or contact us as described in section 1. Anyone on the server can view a player's statistics with `/kauppa stats`. You can also stop all processing by no longer using the bot, and a server administrator can remove the bot from their server.

## 8. Age requirement
The bot is intended for Discord users, and Discord's age requirements apply to it. The minimum age is 13, and it is higher in some countries.

## 9. Security
Apart from game statistics, information is kept only while a game is running. The statistics database and API keys are stored on the bot's server, separately from the public source code. Connections to Discord and Anthropic are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

## 10. Changes
We may update this policy, for example when the bot gets new features. Changes and their history are visible in this file's version history on GitHub. We will announce significant changes before they take effect.
